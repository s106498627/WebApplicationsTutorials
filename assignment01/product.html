FAQ

Are these flash drives compatible with both Mac and PC?
Yes, our flash drives work with Windows, macOS, and Linux computers right out of the box. You do not need to install any special drivers.
What file format do the flash drives use?
They come pre-formatted in exFAT. This format allows you to store files larger than 4GB and read or write files smoothly on both Windows and Mac systems.
What is the read and write speed?
These USB 3.2 drives offer read speeds up to 150 MB/s and write speeds up to 60 MB/s. They transfer large videos and photos in seconds.
Are my files secure on this drive?
The physical drive is durable and water-resistant, but it does not include built-in hardware encryption. You can use standard software like BitLocker or FileVault to password-protect your data.
What kind of warranty comes with the flash drive?
Every drive includes a 5-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures.
